Prüfungsregeln / Exam Rules

  • 🕒
    Zeiteinteilung / Time Limit: Sie haben insgesamt 65 Minuten Zeit (Hören: 20m, Lesen: 25m, Schreiben: 20m). Sprechen is self-paced.
  • 🎧
    Hören (Listening): Dialogues in Part 1 and 3 are played 2 times. Part 2 is played only 1 time.
  • ✍️
    Schreiben (Writing): Consists of form filling and writing an email (30-50 words). Drafts are saved automatically.
  • 🗣️
    Sprechen (Speaking): Speak aloud to prompts. Record, review, and download responses locally.
  • 💾
    Auto-Save: Answers are saved in browser's local storage in real-time. Safe to refresh.
